## Full description

**English**
```
Khwenden is an education directory app. Students can browse universities, institutes,
schools and teachers, view their profiles, and contact them directly.

Features:
• Search educational institutions by city, district, type (public or private) and study level
• Institution profiles with photos, address, departments and contact details
• Find teachers and tutors by subject and city
• Send your CV and apply for jobs published by institutions
• Save favourites and share profiles with others
• Available in Kurdish (Sorani and Badini), Arabic, English and Turkish

Institutions and teachers can create an account, publish their profile and receive
messages from students.

The app is free to use and requires an internet connection.
```

## Words that must NOT appear anywhere (listing, screenshots, icon, in-app, website)

| Avoid | Kurdish / Arabic equivalent |
|---|---|
| best, #1, top, No.1 | باشترین، یەکەمین، لە پێشەوە |
| most modern, fastest, largest | مۆدێرنترین، خێراترین، گەورەترین |
| trusted, leading, official platform | متمانەپێکراو، پێشەنگ |
| award, app of the year | خەڵات، براوە |
| "millions of users", download counts | "ملیۆنان بەکارهێنەر" |

The old sentence *"…together in one **trusted** platform"* was rejected for exactly this
reason — **trusted** is a quality claim, not a description of functionality.
